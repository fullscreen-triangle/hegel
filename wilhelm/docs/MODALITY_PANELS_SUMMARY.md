## Key Insights

### 1. All Modalities Couple to O₂ Clock

Every modality shows synchronization with the O₂ master clock (ω_O2 ≈ 1 kHz):
- **Acoustic**: Resonance peaks at harmonics
- **Thermal**: Temperature oscillations from ATP cycles
- **Electromagnetic**: Field oscillations from electron cascades
- **Mechanical**: Strain waves from membrane deformation
- **Chemical**: Reaction rate modulation from substrate oscillations

### 2. Different Length Scales

Each modality operates at a characteristic length scale:
- **Electromagnetic**: Shortest (Debye screening, ~10 nm)
- **Chemical**: Short (reaction-diffusion, ~0.1 μm)
- **Thermal**: Medium (diffusion, ~0.3 μm)
- **Acoustic**: Medium (wavelength, ~2 μm)
- **Mechanical**: Longest (shear wave, ~1 mm >> cell size)

### 3. Different Propagation Mechanisms

- **Acoustic**: Wave propagation (pressure)
- **Thermal**: Diffusive propagation (heat)
- **Electromagnetic**: Field propagation (screened)
- **Mechanical**: Wave propagation (shear)
- **Chemical**: Reaction-diffusion (nonlinear)

### 4. Compartmentalization Effects

Compartment boundaries (τ_comp ≈ 0.5 ms) affect each modality differently:
- **Acoustic**: Impedance mismatch → partial reflection
- **Thermal**: Rapid equilibration → no boundary effect
- **Electromagnetic**: Charge redistribution → field changes
- **Mechanical**: Stress concentration → deformation
- **Chemical**: Concentration jump → gradient formation

### 5. Timescale Hierarchy

Electromagnetic field propagation: ~10⁻¹⁵ s (instantaneous)
Acoustic wave transit: ~10⁻⁹ s (nanoseconds)
Thermal diffusion: ~10⁻⁴ s (0.1 ms)
